## REP AI

**The Agentic Commerce OS for Shopify brands.** One behavioral brain, every channel.

REP AI sells, supports and engages shoppers on your store, email, Instagram, Facebook and WhatsApp, and brings what it learns back to your team inside the AI assistant you already use.

### What we build

- **AI sales agent** that guides shoppers to the right product and increases conversion
- **AI customer support** that resolves questions and support email across channels
- **[REP AI MCP server](https://github.com/hellorep/rep-ai-mcp)** that connects your REP AI store data to Claude and other MCP clients

### REP AI MCP server

Ask Claude how REP is performing, what shoppers ask about and where they get stuck. Spot a wrong answer? Ask Claude to fix it. Nothing goes live until you approve it.

```bash
claude mcp add --transport http rep-ai https://mcp.hellorep.ai/mcp/v1
```

Or find **REP AI** in the [Claude Connectors Directory](https://claude.ai/directory/rep-ai). Docs and client configs: [hellorep/rep-ai-mcp](https://github.com/hellorep/rep-ai-mcp).
